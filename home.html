DeadKey

A private encrypted vault for Android. Protect photos, videos, documents, notes, and other files on your device without advertising, analytics, or a DeadKey account.

Encrypted by design

Vault contents and metadata are encrypted locally. DeadKey includes optional biometric unlock, privacy-focused display modes, and encrypted backup and recovery tools.

Cloud backup is optional

Users can keep encrypted backups on their device or voluntarily connect Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, or Dropbox. DeadKey encrypts each backup before it leaves the device. Korelis Labs does not operate an intermediary backup server.
